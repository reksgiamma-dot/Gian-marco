    Biography

    Gian Marco Righi, aka Reks

    2009first DJ set
    2015the joystick show is born
    1°DJ to play with a joystick

    Born in Modena, Italy, in 1993, Gian Marco Righi started DJing in 2009 with one clear idea: not to be a DJ like any other. Music, showmanship and nerd culture became the ingredients of a one-of-a-kind career.

    In 2015 he built software that lets him control the decks with a PlayStation controller. The DJ set becomes an interactive show: the crowd doesn't just listen, it interacts with the music and can even play live on stage.

    Hip Hop, Trap, EDM and today's hits blend with the all-time classics that keep everyone dancing. From Peter Pan and Samsara in Riccione to Carroponte in Milan and Papaya in Croatia, he brings the same wow effect everywhere.
